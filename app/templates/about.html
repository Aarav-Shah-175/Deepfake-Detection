Model Footprint

Architecture Overview

The detector utilizes MobileNetV3-Small with pre-trained weights from ImageNet-1K. The classifier head was tailored with a 1024-dimensional hidden layer, Hardswish activation, dropout, and a single linear logit output trained with Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits (BCEWithLogitsLoss).

Training & Validation

  • Training Set: Over 140,000 augmented facial crop samples
  • Validation Set: 39,428 samples
  • Test Set: 10,905 unseen benchmark images
  • Optimizer: Adam with learning rate 1e-4 across 20 full training epochs

Inference Pipeline

Uploaded videos and images are parsed frame-by-frame. Faces are detected and normalized to 224×224 RGB tensors, evaluated through the neural network, and aggregated to produce an accurate, tamper-proof verdict with confidence metrics.
